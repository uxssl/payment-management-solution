Questions? Look Here.

Answers to common questions schools ask before moving fee collection online.

  • What is the Education Payment Management Solution?

    It is a centralized platform for educational institutions to manage fees, digital payments, student information and payment-related communication.

  • Who can use the platform?

    The solution is designed around educational institutions, administrators, students and guardians.

  • Can guardians pay fees online?

    Yes — through cards, mobile banking and net banking.

  • Which payment methods are supported?

    • Visa, Mastercard and other cards

    • bKash, Nagad, Rocket and other mobile banking services

    • Direct payments through leading banks via net banking

  • Is there a mobile application?

    Yes. The solution includes applications for both Android and iOS.

  • Is there a web portal?

    Yes. The solution includes a responsive web interface for administrators and students.

  • Can students and guardians see their payment history?

    Yes. The Student / Guardian Portal allows users to see dues, payments and transaction histories.

  • Can schools manage student and fee information?

    Yes. The School Portal provides administrative control for student records, fee structures, user roles, system settings and reports.

  • Does the platform support notifications?

    Yes. The solution includes an automated notification system using SMS and email.

  • Can the platform track academic and attendance information?

    The unified platform can track student academic performance, attendance and payment records.

  • What technology does the platform use?

    The technology stack includes Flutter, React.js / Material UI, PHP (Laravel / Core PHP), RESTful APIs, MySQL, Firebase Cloud Messaging, SMS Gateway integration and the SSLCOMMERZ payment gateway.

  • Is the platform secure?

    It is presented as a secure platform and identifies role-based access controls.
