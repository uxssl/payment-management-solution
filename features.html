Platform Features

Everything you need to manage school payments

Education Payments brings fee management, digital payments, student information and communication together in one connected platform built for schools and colleges across Bangladesh.

From centralized fee setup to guardian self-service on web and mobile, every module below is designed to reduce manual coordination and give each stakeholder the visibility they need.

The Complete Solution

One platform covering fee administration, digital collection and day-to-day school operations

Centralized Fee Administration

Set up fees, track collection and monitor outstanding dues from one dedicated module

Digital Fee Collection

An integrated payment gateway with cards, mobile banking and net banking support

Web & Mobile Access

Portal and mobile apps that give students and guardians their information on demand

01. Centralized Fee Management

Manage student fees from one dedicated module. Centralize fee calculation, collection and tracking to make day-to-day fee administration more structured and easier to monitor.

  • Outstanding fee visibility

  • Centralized fee-related information
